I went birdwatching at the country park in the morning. I think I arrived a little late, because I didn't see anything particularly rare. The only unfamiliar sight was probably a small white bird that happened to fly past. The rest were moorhens, mallards, doves, and the like, birds I can also see in Beijing.

Unexpectedly, a good buddy of mine was at the other end of the park. I booked a ride to pick him up, intending to go straight to Zhujiajiao's old town together. But the driver seemed to treat the intermediate stop as the final destination. He had me get out where I was picking up my friend, then went off to take another booking. My friend reminded me to check the fare, and I found I'd been charged for the entire trip. I suspected the driver might be playing dumb, so I filed a complaint. In the end, I got a full refund and a few ride vouchers too.

Some parts of Zhujiajiao's old town really do have that Jiangnan water-town feeling, with little bridges and flowing water. But it's also very commercial and international. There was a constant stream of visitors from China and abroad. The houses with their red brick and old roof tiles were mostly restaurants and shops, English signs were posted outside all sorts of businesses, and even entering a temple required a ticket... It felt as though an idealized Jiangnan old town had been constructed, but without a real sense of history.

Walking into the nearby residential area, I found the houses sealed up and marked for demolition. They weren't particularly attractive, and looked like a village from several decades ago. Still, demolishing this genuine history from a few decades back to build an imagined Ming- or Qing-era Jiangnan town felt rather sad to contemplate.

Toward evening, I looked at rentals near the office. The old, shabby, cramped apartments I saw felt even worse than those in Beijing. The interiors might have been renovated, but the stairwells were mostly dark and narrow, and the residential compounds weren't in great shape either. Sigh. It felt like the shadow side of the city's gleaming commercial districts.

In the evening, I met a female friend for Cantonese food, and we played Overcooked together. It was a pretty happy evening overall, even though I'm already bad at games, and being tired made my play even worse.
