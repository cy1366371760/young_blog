Once again, I went straight from work to apartment hunting. I thought I'd found a really nice place, a loft that felt a bit like student housing. But my parents told me to give it some more thought, and they did point out a few drawbacks: everything is on the small side, including the desk, chairs, and bed, and going to the bathroom means a trip up and down the stairs.

It seems that sometimes, resisting the urge to decide right away takes a kind of self-discipline too. I need to make myself remember that I still have a month—there's no need to rush into renting a place. It's the quiet season for rentals, after all, so as a tenant I can afford to be in the wait-and-see camp for now.

Spending so much time making a choice before I really need to feels a bit wasteful. I could have done more with that time. Something to watch out for in the future...
