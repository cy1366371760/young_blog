I met my mentor for yakitori across the street from the office, and we talked about things like changing jobs. He wants to keep working steadily at the company for the long haul. I felt he was also encouraging me to stay a few more years, and he told me to speak to him directly whenever something wasn't going well at work.

By comparison, I often find myself thinking about changing jobs, or even retiring outright. Maybe I find the work in China's quant industry boring and meaningless, or maybe being single and on my own makes me want to take it easy. For now, though, I'll keep going. Relocating to Shanghai is a big change in itself, almost half a job change in a way. There should be new experiences ahead.

The meal was quite good overall. I drank more than half a glass of ginger-flavored beer, and both the yakitori and the udon were tasty. Still, there's an age gap, as well as a workplace hierarchy between us. We never quite relaxed completely into the conversation, and there were occasional awkward silences. A small blemish on an otherwise good meal.

Before heading home that evening, I asked my mentor to play LoL. I'd uninstalled it again after playing and reflecting a few days earlier, so I started downloading it as soon as I got home. Then my mentor said he was going to watch a show with his wife and wouldn't be joining. So happy, winning at life. Since I'd already downloaded it, I figured I'd play a little. Before I knew it, I was up until four in the morning again.

Failed at that one... Sigh. I still need to work on my self-control.
