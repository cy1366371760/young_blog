Problem

  • Platform: AtCoder Beginners Selection
  • Problem: ABC086A - Product
  • Link: AtCoder problem statement

Given two positive integers, determine whether their product is even or odd.

Idea

A product is even exactly when its remainder modulo 2 is zero. Computing the product first makes the condition direct and readable.

Algorithm

  • Read a and b.
  • Compute a * b.
  • Print Even when the product is divisible by 2; otherwise print Odd.

The running time and extra space are both constant.

Reflection

For value equality in OCaml, use a single equals sign: =. The operator == tests physical equality, meaning whether two values are represented by the same object. It happens to agree with = for immediate values such as integers, but it is the wrong default for comparing values.

OCaml Implementation

open Core

let () =
  Scanf.scanf "%d %d" (fun a b ->
    let product = a * b in
    if product mod 2 = 0 then printf "Even\n"
    else printf "Odd\n")
