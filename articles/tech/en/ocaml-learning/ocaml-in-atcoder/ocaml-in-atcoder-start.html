This subcategory will collect AtCoder problem notes solved in OCaml.

The goal is not only to archive accepted code, but also to make the thinking visible: what the key observation was, where parsing or performance mattered, and how the final implementation can stay small and typed.

Planned Note Format

  • Problem metadata: contest, problem link, difficulty, and tags.
  • Core idea: the invariant, reduction, or data-structure choice.
  • Algorithm: enough detail to rederive the implementation.
  • OCaml implementation: full code, plus small notes on library choices.
  • Reflection: mistakes, edge cases, and reusable patterns.

Initial Topics

  • Fast input helpers for AtCoder-style stdin.
  • Arrays, maps, sets, and priority queues in OCaml.
  • Greedy proofs in a compact English style.
  • Translating imperative contest solutions into readable OCaml.
