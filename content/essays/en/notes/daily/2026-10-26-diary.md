Today I took my Nikon 950 to nearby Xiangheyuan Park to photograph the moon. It really is impressive: I could even capture the mottled texture of the lunar surface. At such high magnification, though, it's still hard to keep my hands steady.

I'm counting today as day two of quitting League of Legends. I didn't play, though I did skip through a few clips from Worlds. I spent relatively little time scrolling through short videos, but still watched some longer ones.

I'm going to see the doctor tomorrow afternoon, so an early night tonight! Let's see if I can get up early and find somewhere to wander around in the morning.
