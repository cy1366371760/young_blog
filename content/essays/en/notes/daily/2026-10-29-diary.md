Today I listened to my manager share how to squeeze every last bit out of a $100 personal GPT subscription. I learned that you can wrap a website in an app, so I immediately had GPT turn my personal blog into an Android app and put it on my phone.

There were a few other tips too, such as adding buttons that call AI. One idea is a button that sends AI through all the app's backend logs to debug and fix problems automatically. That could be something to try in the future.

I was pretty unmotivated after work, perhaps because the operations work had left my head spinning a bit. So I played through another two CLANNAD routes, mainly involving the second male lead. My takeaway: apparently friendship can have that cheesy whiff of romance too.

Then I practiced the moonwalk again and noticed a few new things. It looks like a smooth, frictionless glide, but actually doing it seems to work best with plenty of grip. That's what keeps the foot on tiptoe planted, and it takes quite a bit of leg strength too.

Using enough strength to create lightness, producing an illusion of the exact opposite. Pretty interesting!
