I'm in Shanghai on a work trip. At lunchtime, I went to a restaurant for a bowl of seafood noodles. Pretty good. Eating in is still better than ordering delivery.

I didn't do much after that, just watched quite a few videos about the election campaign in Taipei. It felt like the familiar pattern: the side in office points to its achievements, while the opposition offers grand promises.

In the evening, I booked tickets and a hotel for Suzhou, then kept browsing rental listings in Shanghai. Serviced apartments look quite nice, but they're a little too expensive. Something between a serviced apartment and an ordinary rental seems almost impossible to find; the other options are old, shabby, cramped flats. That's quite a jump between the choices...
