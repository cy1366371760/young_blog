I paid the rental deposit today, so the apartment hunt should be settled for now.

In the evening, we went go-karting for a team outing. I was terrible at it. I was a little too nervous about the corners that came one after another, so I kept braking and almost coming to a stop to get through them. Proudly claimed last place.

Afterward, we went to Chaimen Hui, a Sichuan restaurant with one Michelin star, for dinner. It turned out we hadn't booked enough tables, so four of us took a table and ordered our food. It basically felt like Sichuan flavors with premium ingredients. Fairly standard, but decent enough.
