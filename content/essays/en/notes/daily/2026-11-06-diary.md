Had seafood noodles for lunch. Pretty good. I've barely ordered delivery during this work trip to Shanghai. Food freshly made at a restaurant really is better.

After work, I headed to Suzhou for a little weekend trip. At eleven at night, I went out for a walk around the Shantang scenic area. There were lots of stalls and people selling snacks. The night scenery really had the feel of an old Jiangnan town: soft yellow lights against white walls and blue-gray tiled roofs. Music coming from the bars did break the spell a little, though.

Before heading back, I bought a fifteen-yuan portion of Northeastern-style chicken frames. They were braised with chilies, Sichuan peppercorns, and spices like those in Chinese five-spice. So fragrant.

While eating, I watched a video about the origins of the snack, which talked about the wave of layoffs at state-owned enterprises back then. Starting with a simple snack, I learned a little more about a painful chapter of history. It was quite moving.
