Today didn't go very well. Last night, I stayed up watching videos until two again. I woke up fairly early this morning and should have gone back to sleep, but I couldn't resist watching the LoL tournament instead...

That set the tone for a whole day of drifting. After the matches, I downloaded Hearthstone and played that. Even while visiting the Lingering Garden, my mind was elsewhere, and I didn't really take it in. On the train, I played FGO. After getting back to Beijing, I played a little Clannad, then went back to LoL. My resolution to quit LoL and PvP games lasted two weeks before I broke it. Sigh.

It's past midnight now, and I'm feeling low again. Thinking about Clannad's story and comparing it with my own reality, I'm not quite sure why I'm trying so hard, or why I'm working. The industry I'm in feels like it has nothing beyond money. I'm on my own, and I don't know what I'm earning it for. The world feels vast and empty. Sigh.

Thinking about how I got here, I feel I still haven't given sleep and a regular routine enough priority. When I haven't slept enough and feel foggy, it's easy to slip into this state. I need to keep trying to get my body and my sleep routine into better shape. Maybe then even the simple act of breathing will feel refreshing and freeing.

I hope I can live well.
