I've been playing the anime-style mobile game FGO for ten years. Recently, I got someone else to do the grinding and advance the main story for me, while I went to Bilibili to watch the corresponding story recap and commentary videos. I've found that this approach saves time and makes the story more enjoyable: without all the boring battles in between, the narrative feels much more coherent.

My reading of this part of the story is that its theme isn't pursuing fairness, but protecting the people you care about most. Pretty good.

After work, I spent pretty much the whole evening playing LoL. There was some little event offering a skin for three wins, and I fell for it. Sigh. Back to square one!

All in all, a day of mostly slacking off.
