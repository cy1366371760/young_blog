I told my mentor I'd be moving to Shanghai next weekend and mentioned that I'd like to treat him to a meal. Some things feel so difficult before you say them, but once they're out, the conversation feels quite natural. No big deal, really. He also shared some gossip about colleagues changing jobs. Maybe we can talk more about that when we meet for a meal.

I also signed up for a company volunteer activity the weekend after next, spending a day with visually impaired children. I wanted to join as soon as I saw it. I'm not sure whether I truly want to do something charitable, simply want the experience, or want to witness other people's sadness in real life. But with things like this, what counts is what you do, rather than what's in your heart. Showing up and doing my best is something worth recognizing.

In the evening, I watched videos, played FGO, then did a little overtime, and ended up going to bed late again. I thought my new sleep mask might improve my sleep quality, but it didn't. I woke up quite early, hungry and unable to get back to sleep.

Seems I still need to go to bed earlier...
