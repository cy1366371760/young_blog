I finished all of Clannad's main storylines, then watched its anime adaptation as well.

What I took from it is roughly this: happiness and sadness always grow alongside each other. When we experience real happiness at a particular moment, we spend the days afterward hoping it will last, or at least seeking something like it.

But as the days pass, everything around us keeps changing and moving forward. We can only change ourselves to adapt. Sometimes, though, change comes too quickly. Something beautiful can vanish in an instant, like a life.

Then grief we cannot let go of sweeps over us like a tide. Precisely because we have those happy memories, the sadness becomes all the more suffocating. Faced with changes too difficult to bear, people often sink into despondency and face life behind a mask of numbness.

At its root, time brings change, letting reality drift, quickly or slowly, away from our memories of happiness. The antidote may be to wait through a still longer stretch of time, until this present that has strayed from happiness also becomes the past, and we find new, small joys to hold up our hearts again.

Beyond these thoughts, I also realized how much I like stories that bring me to tears. Sadness feels all the more precious in this era. It gives me a sense of my heart being cleansed and washed anew.
