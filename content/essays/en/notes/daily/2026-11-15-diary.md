I went for a walk in Fragrant Hills Park on Sunday afternoon. The subway station was packed. The scenery was lovely, with hills and clear water, and leaves in red, green, and yellow.

People bustled about, and quite a few tour groups were taking photos together. Nearby shops sold the grilled sausages you find at just about every tourist attraction. The prices were fairly reasonable.

I looked through the [park's introduction](https://www.xiangshan-park.cn/cn/tabs/showdetail.aspx?selectsingle=1&tabid=700002) and learned that building here began in the Jin dynasty, more than eight hundred years ago. Another major period of construction came during the Qianlong reign, two or three centuries ago.

Then again, two or three centuries amount to only a few turns of a white-haired elder meeting a young child, one generation giving way to the next. A thought that stayed with me.
