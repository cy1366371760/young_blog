I watched Chai Jing tell the story of Hu Shih and his son, Hu Sidu. It left me thinking: like father, like son.

At first, I thought Hu Sidu's choice to stay in China was somewhat blind. Later, seeing him stand up and say what he truly thought changed how I felt. Newspapers criticized him for carrying on Hu Shih's legacy. But viewed over a longer span of history, I felt that this very point showed who had truly been on the right side.

A few other thoughts of my own after watching:

- Where people have no freedom to remain silent, disasters will follow one after another.
- Freedom should be loved more than bread, more than life itself.
- Those who advocate sweeping reform always end up becoming dictators, because they allow no dissenting voices.
- We should oppose every theory that permits neither criticism nor questioning.
