It's been a while since I last wrote a diary entry.

Last week, I got absorbed in LoL and played every day. It feels like coming back after trying to quit has only made the gaming habit worse. Withdrawal symptoms, apparently (ha).

Yesterday, Sunday, I moved to Shanghai. The online joke is that I've gone from "semi-feudal" to "semi-colonial," but it doesn't feel all that different. More like moving from one concrete-and-steel jungle to another.

Today is my twenty-fifth birthday, and I hadn't expected to spend the evening working overtime. But more than half of what caused the problem was my own doing, so there wasn't much else to do. Once you're an adult out in the working world, you have to take responsibility for your mistakes. You're no longer a kid at school with someone to catch you when things go wrong. Still, having this happen on my birthday left me a little wistful.

I've also been obsessed with Luo Dayou's songs lately, listening nonstop during the day and singing them at night. Many of his songs are cynical, chronicling history and criticizing different societies. They're powerful and deeply moving. As it happens, a friend has been introducing me to vinyl records, so tomorrow I'm planning to browse some record shops and look for his albums. Though I have a feeling quite a few of them may be banned (ha).
